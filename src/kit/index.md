A Google connection for a personal assistant. Kit can use Gmail and Google Calendar to answer the account owner's requests, find messages, check calendars, and perform requested mail or calendar actions within the permissions granted during Google sign-in.

This is a personal-use integration, not a public signup service. Its OAuth client runs on the owner's computer. The public website provides information only; it does not receive the localhost authorization callback or collect Google credentials.
