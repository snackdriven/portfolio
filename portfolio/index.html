About

My professional QA work covers healthcare workflows, care-module test strategy, and recurring release readiness and handoffs. I also turn broad investigations into prioritized readouts for product and engineering.

With 6+ years in QA, I turn recurring headaches into practical workflows and build the tools I wish existed. Sometimes that’s a Kanban board. Sometimes it’s a haunted craft shop with slightly unruly vines.

For my personal builds, AI writes the code. I define the behavior and acceptance checks, direct implementation, and reject results the evidence doesn’t support. Then I pick at it until it works the way it should.
