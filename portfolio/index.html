About

I build quality infrastructure, not just test cases. Eight behavioral health products, no staging on the legacy platform, 350+ runtime-generated form fields: instead of doing the same manual work forever, I built a config-driven Playwright harness with LLM-assisted selector resolution plus 14 internal tools. Documentation down to an hour a week, 63-section forms to one-click autofill. I direct AI coding agents the way I run test strategy: point at the friction, verify the output, ship what survives. The tools are documented and config-driven on purpose: shared infrastructure, not personal scripts.
