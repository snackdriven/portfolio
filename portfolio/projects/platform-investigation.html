01Problem

Ahead of launch decisions, the team needed staging findings sorted by data risk, related failures, and open product questions.

I prioritized the risks and showed where coverage stopped.

02Decisions

I directed AI-assisted browser harnesses across application sections, admin settings, and cross-page behavior. I checked beyond Save: refreshes, stale forms, repeated actions, and changes elsewhere.

I grouped 250 findings into 23 patterns, separating observations from intended-behavior questions. Five decisions went to product and engineering owners.

03Validation

I repeated checks and used a second review to challenge higher-impact findings. Configuration checks led to withdrawals; one result became flaky on retest. Stored-data claims required save and readback.

The footage audit separated full matches, partial support, and unrecorded steps. A Save click alone did not prove persistence.
